# Processed data

The repository includes the processed, event-aligned CSV tensors used in the
paper so the reported analyses can be reproduced without access to the raw
acquisition files. Each event has balanced ictal/nonictal labels and 5,376
samples per signal (21 seconds at 256 Hz) for EEG, EMG, and photometry.

of, or grant rights beyond those already held for, the underlying experimental
data. Users remain responsible for complying with applicable institutional,
ethical, consent, and data-use requirements. Do not attempt to re-identify
subjects or infer omitted raw-data provenance.
